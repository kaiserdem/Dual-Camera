Privacy Policy

DuoPrompter · Last updated: 4 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DuoPrompter (“the App”) handles your information. Contact: nikolovalena1616@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • The App records video and photos using your device’s front and back cameras.
  • Media you capture is saved to your Photos library on your device.
  • Teleprompter scripts and app settings are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not operate user accounts or upload your recordings to our servers.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Information the App accesses

Depending on how you use the App, it may access:

  • Camera — live preview and capture (front and back, including multi-camera mode on supported devices).
  • Microphone — audio when you record video (if you allow microphone access).
  • Photo Library (Add Only) — to save photos and videos you create.
  • Motion sensors — optional leveler overlay (device orientation).

The App does not require location access for core features.

3. Data stored on your device

  • Teleprompter script text (if you use the teleprompter)
  • Layout, appearance, and export preferences
  • Photos and videos you save — in the iOS Photos library, not on our servers

Deleting the App removes its local settings and teleprompter text from the device (saved media in Photos remains unless you delete it separately).

4. Data we collect on our servers

None. DuoPrompter does not require sign-in and does not send your recordings, scripts, or camera content to the developer’s servers for storage or processing.

5. Third parties

Apple provides the operating system, App Store distribution, and (if you use them in the future) in-app purchase processing. Apple’s privacy terms apply to those services.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use it if you are under 13.

7. Your responsibilities when recording

You are responsible for obtaining consent from people you record and for complying with local laws about photography and video recording.

8. Your choices

  • You can deny Camera, Microphone, or Photos permissions; some features will not work.
  • You can clear teleprompter text and reset settings in the App.
  • You can delete saved media in the Photos app at any time.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions: nikolovalena1616@gmail.com
